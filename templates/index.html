Clinical Study Protocol & Deep Learning Framework

Framingham Heart Study: One of the most authoritative prospective cardiovascular epidemiological studies in medical history. The model uses 15 clinical parameters across 4,240 patient records to estimate 10-year risk of developing Coronary Heart Disease (`TenYearCHD`).

PyTorch Neural Network Architecture: 4 dense layers (15 → 64 → 32 → 16 → 1) with Batch Normalization, Dropout ($0.3/0.2/0.1$), and weighted Binary Cross-Entropy Loss ($w_{\text{pos}} = 5.59$) to overcome the natural 84.8% vs 15.2% class imbalance and maximize clinical screening sensitivity.

Clinical Guidelines Integration: Risk alerts evaluate blood pressure against standard AHA/ACC 2024 hypertension classifications, lipid risk criteria ($>240$ mg/dL), and glycemic thresholds ($>126$ mg/dL).
